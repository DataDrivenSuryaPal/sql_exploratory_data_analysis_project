# 📊 SQL Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project demonstrates **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) using SQL Server** to analyze customer, product, and sales data and uncover meaningful business insights.

The analysis focuses on understanding the structure of the data, calculating key business metrics, identifying top and low-performing products and customers, analyzing sales patterns, and creating a reusable **product performance report**.

The project is designed to demonstrate practical **T-SQL, data analysis, aggregation, joins, CTEs, window functions, conditional logic, and SQL views**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

* Explore customer and product dimensions.
* Understand the distribution of customers and products.
* Calculate important business KPIs and measures.
* Analyze sales, revenue, quantity, and customer performance.
* Identify top and lowest-performing products.
* Identify the highest-revenue customers.
* Analyze product performance and segmentation.
* Build a reusable product-level reporting view.
* Demonstrate practical SQL techniques used in data analytics.

## 🔍 Exploratory Data Analysis

### 1. Dimension Exploration

The dimension exploration script examines the available customer and product dimensions.

Key analysis includes:

* Identifying the countries customers come from.
* Exploring product categories.
* Exploring product subcategories.
* Listing products within each category.

The analysis uses the `gold.dim_customers` and `gold.dim_products` tables.

---

### 2. Measures Exploration

The measures analysis calculates important high-level business metrics from the sales and dimension tables.

### Key Metrics

* **Total Sales**
* **Total Items Sold**
* **Average Selling Price**
* **Total Orders**
* **Total Products**
* **Total Customers**

These metrics provide a quick overview of the overall business performance.

---

### 3. Magnitude Analysis

Magnitude analysis is used to understand the size and distribution of different business dimensions.

The analysis includes:

* Total customers by country.
* Total products by category.
* Customer distribution by gender.
* Average product cost by category.
* Total revenue generated by each category.
* Total revenue generated by each customer.
* Total quantity of products sold by country.

This analysis combines the `gold.fact_sales`, `gold.dim_customers`, and `gold.dim_products` tables to generate business-level insights.

---

### 4. Ranking Analysis

Ranking analysis identifies the best and worst performers across products and customers.

The project analyzes:

* Top 5 products by revenue.
* Bottom 5 products by revenue.
* Top 10 customers by revenue.
* Customers with the fewest orders.
* Product rankings using the `ROW_NUMBER()` window function.

These queries help identify high-value products and customers as well as areas that may require further investigation.

## 📈 Product Performance Report

One of the main components of the project is the creation of a reusable SQL view called:

```sql

```

The report consolidates product-level information and performance metrics into a single analytical view.

### Product Information

The report contains:

* Product name
* Category
* Subcategory
* Product cost
* Last order date

### Product Performance Metrics

It calculates:

* Product lifespan
* Recency
* Total orders
* Total sales
* Total quantity sold
* Total unique customers
* Average selling price
* Average order revenue
* Average monthly revenue

## 🏷️ Product Segmentation

Products are classified into three performance segments based on total sales:

| Segment           |         Revenue |
| ----------------- | --------------: |
| 🟢 High Performer |        > 50,000 |
| 🟡 Mid-Range      | 10,000 – 50,000 |
| 🔴 Low Performer  |        < 10,000 |

This segmentation makes it easier to identify products that are driving revenue and products that may need additional attention.
